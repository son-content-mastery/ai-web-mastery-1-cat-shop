# Information architecture

- `/`: discovery homepage targeting อาหารแมว.
- `/products/`: all products and filters.
- `/products/[slug]/`: product detail and LINE conversion.
- `/categories/cat-food/`, `/categories/cat-litter/`: category discovery.
- `/brands/royal-canin/`, `/brands/maxima/`: brand discovery.
- `/blog/`, `/blog/[slug]/`: editorial listing and articles.
- `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/privacy/`, `/purchasing/`: trust and service information.
- `/404.html`, `/rss.xml`, sitemap and robots: discovery support.

Navigation and footer link to the main branches. Breadcrumbs connect child pages to their parent; articles and product pages cross-link related content.
