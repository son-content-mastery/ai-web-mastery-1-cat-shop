# Content and SEO

Each route has a unique title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card, one H1, semantic landmarks, useful links, and descriptive alt text. Astro generates `/sitemap.xml` from the fixed routes and content collections; `robots.txt` and RSS are present. Keyword wording is natural and supporting information sits after primary product UX. Do not add fake Review/AggregateRating, availability, stock, medical, certification, or customer-count schema.
